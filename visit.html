Before you arrive

Plan a smooth visit

Gorakhpur Zoo is spread across 25 acres with shaded walkways, resting points and drinking water facilities. A little planning makes the day easier.

  • Book tickets online to skip the entrance queue
  • Wear comfortable walking shoes and carry a water bottle
  • Arrive early to see animals during their active hours
  • Check the gate board for keeper talks and feeding times
  • Carry a cap and sunscreen during summer months

How to reach

Getting here is easy

Gorakhpur is well connected by rail, road and air. The zoo is a short ride from the main railway station.

By train

Gorakhpur Junction is about 4 km away. Autos and e-rickshaws are available outside the station.

By bus

UPSRTC buses stop near Taramandal. Local shared autos connect the bus stand to the zoo gate.

By air

Gorakhpur Airport is around 8 km from the zoo. Taxis take about 20 minutes.

By car

Paid parking is available for cycles, two-wheelers, cars, autos and buses for up to 4 hours.

Accessibility

  • Wheelchair-friendly paths across the main route
  • Ramps at ticketing and main attractions
  • Accessible washrooms near the entrance
  • Staff assistance available at the gate

Visitor tips

  • Last entry is at 4:30 PM
  • Outside food is allowed in designated picnic areas
  • Plastic bags and single-use plastic are discouraged
  • Keep your ticket until you leave the zoo
