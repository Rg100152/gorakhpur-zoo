Privacy Policy

How Gorakhpur Zoo handles information when you use this website, book a ticket or contact us.

Last updated: 1 January 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Gorakhpur Zoo website ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you browse the site, make a demonstration booking or send us a message. This website is a static demonstration project and does not operate a live ticketing or payment system.

1. Information we collect

We only collect information that you choose to provide through the website forms. This may include:

  • Name, phone number and email address submitted through booking, contact, feedback or event forms.
  • Visit date, visitor counts, booking type and calculated ticket totals for demonstration bookings.
  • Feedback category, rating and message content.
  • Newsletter email address if you choose to subscribe.

2. How information is stored

This website does not send form data to a server. Demo bookings, contact messages, feedback and newsletter sign-ups are saved in your browser's localStorage so the site can show confirmations and prevent duplicate demo bookings. The data remains on your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser data.

If you use a public or shared device, please avoid entering personal information and clear your browser data after use.

3. How we use information

When form data is submitted, it is used to:

  • Display a demonstration booking confirmation and prevent duplicate bookings for the same date and phone number.
  • Show a confirmation message for contact, feedback and event forms.
  • Understand visitor preferences and improve the website and services.

4. Third-party services

This website loads resources from third parties that may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type and referring page. These include:

  • Google Fonts for typography.
  • Unsplash for photography displayed on the site.
  • Google Maps for the embedded location map on the visit and contact pages.

These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

The website itself does not set advertising cookies. Third-party resources such as embedded maps and fonts may set cookies or use similar technologies according to their own policies. Your browser settings allow you to block or delete cookies.

6. Sharing information

We do not sell or rent your information. Because form submissions are stored locally in your browser, we do not automatically share them with third parties. If you contact us directly by email or phone, normal communication and record-keeping practices apply.

7. Data retention

Information saved in localStorage remains until you clear it or your browser removes it. To delete demo data, clear site data for this website in your browser settings.

8. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have shared with us through direct contact channels. For data stored in your browser, you can delete it yourself using browser settings. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

9. Children's privacy

This website is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Booking forms should be completed by a parent, guardian or responsible adult.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no website or storage method is completely secure. Please do not submit sensitive information such as payment details or identity documents through the demo forms.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page with an updated date.
