Butterfly Park

A quiet, planted garden where colourful butterflies flutter freely around visitors.

Nature

Butterfly Park

The Butterfly Park is a living garden filled with nectar plants, host plants and more than 20 butterfly species. It is a peaceful place to slow down and watch nature at work.

Mornings are the best time to visit, when butterflies are most active. The garden also includes an emergence chamber where you can learn how caterpillars transform.

The experience

What to expect

  • More than 20 free-flying butterfly species
  • Host and nectar plants labelled for learning
  • A small emergence chamber
  • Shaded paths and photo-friendly spots
Visitor rules

Please remember

  • Do not touch or catch butterflies
  • Stay on the marked path
  • No nets, jars or collecting equipment
  • Keep voices low to avoid disturbing wildlife
Good to know: Butterfly Park entry is included with your zoo ticket. Butterflies are most active on warm, sunny mornings.
