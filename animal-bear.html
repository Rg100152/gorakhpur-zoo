Meet the Sloth Bear

Melursus ursinus Vulnerable

The sloth bear is a shaggy, long-clawed bear adapted for a diet of insects. Its lips and tongue work like a vacuum to suck termites and ants out of mounds.

Our bear ridge includes natural digging areas and shaded dens. Sloth bears have poor eyesight but an excellent sense of smell, so they often find their enrichment treats before keepers have finished hiding them.

Did you know?

Fun facts

  • Sloth bears use their long curved claws to tear open termite mounds.
  • They can close their nostrils to protect their nose while eating insects.
  • Mothers carry young cubs on their backs.
  • Despite their name, sloth bears are active and can run surprisingly fast.

Conservation matters

Sloth bears are threatened by habitat loss, poaching and the illegal wildlife trade. Rescue and rehabilitation centres play an important role in giving displaced bears a safe home.

Every ticket supports animal care, enrichment and conservation education at Gorakhpur Zoo.
