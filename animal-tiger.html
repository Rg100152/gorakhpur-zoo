Meet the Royal Bengal Tiger

Panthera tigris tigris Endangered

The Royal Bengal Tiger is a symbol of wild India — a solitary, powerful hunter built for stealth. No two tigers share the same stripe pattern, which makes every individual easy to identify.

Our tiger trail is designed around natural cover, a water pool and elevated viewing decks. Look closely at the long grass; the stripes are nature's finest camouflage.

Royal Bengal Tiger facts

Everything you need to know before you visit this enclosure.

Habitat

Tropical forest, mangroves and tall grassland

Where to find

Tiger Trail · east side of the zoo

Diet

Deer, sambar, wild boar and small mammals

Lifespan

15–20 years in human care

Weight

Male 180–260 kg · Female 100–160 kg

Conservation status

Endangered

Did you know?

Fun facts

  • Every tiger has a unique stripe pattern, much like a human fingerprint.
  • Tigers are strong swimmers and often cool off in rivers and pools.
  • A tiger's night vision is about six times sharper than a human's.
  • They can leap more than 5 metres in a single bound.

Conservation matters

Project Tiger has helped stabilise India's tiger population through protected reserves, anti-poaching patrols and community engagement. Zoos support conservation through education and careful breeding programmes.

Every ticket supports animal care, enrichment and conservation education at Gorakhpur Zoo.
