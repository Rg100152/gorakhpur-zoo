Meet the Indian Peacock

Pavo cristatus Least Concern

The Indian peafowl is the national bird of India and one of the most photographed residents at the zoo. Only the male — the peacock — carries the famous train of iridescent feathers.

Our bird walk is planted with flowering shrubs and tall roosting trees. The courtship display is most likely during warm mornings and after light rain.

Did you know?

Fun facts

  • The peacock's train is made of elongated upper-tail covert feathers.
  • Only male peafowl have the iconic train — females are called peahens.
  • Their loud call is often heard before a rain shower.
  • Peafowl roost in tall trees to stay safe from predators at night.

Conservation matters

The Indian peafowl is widely distributed and legally protected in India. Zoos help visitors appreciate the species while promoting coexistence in farmland and urban green spaces.

Every ticket supports animal care, enrichment and conservation education at Gorakhpur Zoo.
