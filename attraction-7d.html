Motion seats, wind, rain and 3D visuals combine for a short, unforgettable adventure.

7D Theatre

The 7D Theatre takes a traditional cinema visit and adds motion, weather and light effects. Seats move with the action while wind, mist and flashes of light make every scene feel real.

Shows run throughout the day and last around 15 minutes. Tickets are separate from zoo entry and available at the theatre counter or online.
