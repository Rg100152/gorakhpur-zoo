Aquarium

Walk beneath glowing tanks and discover freshwater and marine species from across the world.

Marine

Aquarium

Our Marine Discovery Centre brings the underwater world to landlocked Gorakhpur. Glowing display tanks showcase reef fish, native river species and fascinating invertebrates.

The aquarium is designed for slow exploration, with low lighting and interpretive panels at eye level for children. It is a calm, cool stop on a hot afternoon.

The experience

What to expect

  • Freshwater and marine display tanks
  • Native river species of Uttar Pradesh
  • Coral and reef-themed exhibits
  • Interpretive panels designed for young learners
Visitor rules

Please remember

  • Do not tap on the glass
  • No flash photography
  • Do not feed the fish
  • Keep noise to a minimum
Good to know: Aquarium entry is included with your zoo admission ticket. Check the gate board for feeding and talk timings.
