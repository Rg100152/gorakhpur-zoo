More than a job

Every role at Gorakhpur Zoo contributes to animal welfare, conservation and the experience of thousands of visitors each month. If you are curious, dependable and care about wildlife, we would like to hear from you.

  • Meaningful work supporting animal care and conservation
  • A collaborative, safety-first team culture
  • Training and professional development opportunities
  • Staff wellness, leave benefits and a green working environment
