Meet the Spotted Deer

Axis axis Least Concern

The spotted deer, or chital, is one of India's most elegant herbivores. Its white spots stay for life and help break up its outline in dappled forest light.

Our deer meadow is home to a small herd that shares a woodland walk with other gentle species. Listen for the sharp alarm call the herd uses when it senses a threat.

Did you know?

Fun facts

  • Unlike many deer, chital keep their spots throughout their lives.
  • Males grow antlers that are shed and regrown every year.
  • The herd communicates with a short, high-pitched alarm call.
  • Chital often follow langurs to feed on fruit dropped from trees.

Conservation matters

Spotted deer remain widespread, but habitat fragmentation and road accidents are growing concerns. Wildlife corridors and safe road crossings help keep populations connected.
