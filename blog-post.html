5 Things to Know Before Your First Zoo Visit

A little planning makes a big difference. Here is how to get the most from your day at Gorakhpur Zoo.

RG By Raj Gautam · Zoo Team · 15 February 2026 · 6 min read

Your first visit to a zoo can be exciting, overwhelming and a little tiring — all at once. Gorakhpur Zoo covers 25 acres, with animals, attractions, gardens and walking trails to explore. A few simple decisions before you arrive can turn a good day out into a great one.

1. Arrive early

Animals are generally most active in the cooler hours of the morning. Big cats, deer and birds are more likely to be moving, feeding or interacting with enrichment before the midday heat sets in. Aim to reach the gate close to opening time at 9:00 AM.

2. Book your ticket online

Online booking saves time at the entrance and lets you see group discounts instantly. Your ticket includes a QR code that you can show at the gate. Keep a valid photo ID with you, especially if you are using a student or disability discount.

3. Wear the right things

Comfortable walking shoes are essential. The zoo has shaded paths, but you will still cover a lot of ground. A cap, sunglasses and a refillable water bottle make the day much more comfortable, particularly in summer.

Local tip: The hour before closing is often the quietest time to revisit your favourite enclosure — and the light is beautiful for photographs.

4. Plan around the animals, not the map

Check the information board at the gate for keeper talk and feeding times. It is worth planning your route around one or two scheduled activities, then wandering freely. This is especially useful for families with young children who may need breaks.

5. Respect the rules — and the animals

Never feed the animals, tap on glass or enter restricted areas. Stay behind barriers, keep voices low near enclosures and follow instructions from zoo staff. These rules keep both visitors and animals safe.

  • Do not feed any animal, even if it looks hungry.
  • Keep children within arm's reach near enclosures.
  • Do not use flash photography inside the aquarium.
  • Carry your trash to the nearest bin or take it home.

Bonus: make time for the small things

The butterfly garden, aquarium and bird walk are easy to skip when you are rushing between big animals. Slow down. Some of the best moments at the zoo happen in the quiet corners — a butterfly landing nearby, a turtle surfacing, or a child noticing something new.
