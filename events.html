How event entry works

Entry to regular events is included with your zoo ticket. Workshops and after-hours events have limited seats and require advance registration.

  • Buy a regular zoo ticket for daytime events
  • Register online for workshops and special evenings
  • Arrive 15 minutes before the scheduled start
  • Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult
