Zoo Rules

Help us keep the animals, visitors and green spaces safe. A few simple rules make the day better for everyone.

Visitor code of conduct

Do's and don'ts

Please read these before you enter. Staff may ask visitors to leave if these rules are not followed.

Please do

  • Keep your ticket with you until you leave the zoo.
  • Stay on marked paths and behind barriers.
  • Supervise children at all times, especially near water.
  • Use bins or take your waste home with you.
  • Follow instructions from zoo staff and volunteers.
  • Carry a refillable water bottle and stay hydrated.
  • Report any injured animal or unsafe situation to staff immediately.
  • Respect other visitors — keep noise to a reasonable level.

Please don't

  • Feed, touch or tease any animal.
  • Enter enclosures, staff areas or restricted zones.
  • Tap on glass or use flash photography in the aquarium.
  • Bring pets, drones or laser pointers into the zoo.
  • Play loud music, shout or run near enclosures.
  • Pluck flowers, damage plants or climb trees.
  • Smoke, vape or consume alcohol on the premises.
  • Litter, use single-use plastic or light fires.
Good to know

Specific rules by area

Animal enclosures

Never feed animals. Human food can make them seriously ill. Stay behind barriers and keep hands, arms and camera equipment outside enclosures.

Photography

Personal photography requires a ₹100 ticket. No flash in the aquarium. Commercial shoots and drones need prior written permission.

Children & groups

Children must be accompanied by an adult. School groups should stay with their teacher or group leader at all times.

7D Theatre

Not suitable for pregnant women, heart patients or children under 6. Keep hands and feet inside the seat during the show.

Green spaces

Please stay on paths across lawns and garden beds. Do not pick flowers, fruit or leaves. Help us protect the butterfly garden.

Emergency

In an emergency, follow staff instructions and use the nearest exit. First aid is available at the visitor centre.

Important: Gorakhpur Zoo reserves the right to refuse entry or ask visitors to leave if they endanger animals, staff or other visitors. Tickets are non-transferable and non-refundable except as required by law.
