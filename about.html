About Gorakhpur Zoo

A home for wildlife and a place for people — discover our history, mission and the team behind the gates.

Our story

More than a place to visit

Gorakhpur Zoo is a green refuge in the heart of the city — a place where families meet wildlife, children discover the natural world and animals receive dedicated, professional care.

Set across 25 acres of gardens, woodland and naturalistic enclosures, the zoo is home to more than 120 animals across 50 species. Our work combines animal welfare, conservation education and community access.

What drives us

Mission, vision & values

Everything we do is guided by a commitment to animal welfare, conservation and learning.

Our mission

To provide excellent care for animals while inspiring visitors to value and protect wildlife.

Our vision

A city where every child grows up connected to nature, with a zoo that leads by example.

Our values

Animal welfare first, honesty with visitors, respect for our team and care for the environment.

Our journey

A short history

From a small municipal park to a modern wildlife destination, the zoo has grown alongside the city it serves.

Please note: This website is a demonstration project built for Gorakhpur Zoo. Historical details are illustrative.
  • 1980sA small municipal animal park

    The zoo began as a modest animal park with a handful of enclosures and a strong local following.

  • 1990sA growing collection

    New habitats were added for big cats, elephants and native deer, alongside a dedicated veterinary care facility.

  • 2000sEducation takes centre stage

    School programmes, keeper talks and interpretation boards brought conservation learning to the front.

  • 2010sModern attractions

    The aquarium, butterfly park and 7D theatre helped the zoo become a full-day family destination.

  • Today25 acres of green habitat

    Gorakhpur Zoo cares for more than 120 animals across 50 species, with a focus on native wildlife and visitor education.
