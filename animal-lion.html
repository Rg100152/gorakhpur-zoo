Meet the Asiatic Lion

Panthera leo persica Endangered

The Asiatic lion is one of the most recognisable big cats on Earth. Slightly smaller than its African cousin, it carries a distinctive fold of skin along its belly and a shorter, denser mane.

Our pride enjoys a large, shaded savanna-style enclosure with open sight lines, rocky resting points and a shallow pool. The best time to see them active is early morning or just before closing.

Did you know?

Fun facts

  • Lions live in family groups called prides, usually led by related females.
  • A male lion's roar can carry up to 8 kilometres across open country.
  • Lions can sleep for up to 20 hours a day to conserve energy.
  • The Asiatic lion has a characteristic skin fold along its belly.

Conservation matters

Asiatic lions survive in the wild in a single population in Gujarat's Gir Forest. Coordinated zoo breeding programmes help protect genetic diversity and support public awareness about habitat protection.

Every ticket supports animal care, enrichment and conservation education at Gorakhpur Zoo.
