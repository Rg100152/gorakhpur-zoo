Wildlife Week Celebration

Every year, Wildlife Week brings our visitors closer to the animals and the people who care for them. Join us for a packed day of talks, demonstrations and activities designed for curious minds of every age.

Meet the keepers, learn how enrichment keeps animals mentally and physically healthy, and discover simple ways you can help protect wildlife in your own neighbourhood.

Event highlights

  • Live keeper talks at the lion, tiger and elephant enclosures
  • Elephant bath and enrichment demonstration
  • Conservation quiz with prizes for children
  • Guided butterfly garden walk
  • Wildlife-themed art corner for young visitors
Good to know: This event is included with your regular zoo entry ticket. No separate registration is required for the daytime programme. Bring a refillable water bottle and wear comfortable shoes.
