Meet the Asian Elephant

Elephas maximus Endangered

Asian elephants are among the most intelligent animals on the planet. They live in close family groups, communicate through low-frequency rumbles and are known for their remarkable memory.

Our elephant enclosure includes a mud wallow, a dust-bathing area and a shallow pool. The daily bath and enrichment session is a favourite with visitors — check the board at the gate for timings.

Did you know?

Fun facts

  • An elephant's trunk contains roughly 40,000 muscles.
  • Elephants can communicate using infrasound too low for humans to hear.
  • A large adult can eat up to 150 kg of plant matter in a day.
  • Elephants are known to recognise individuals after many years apart.

Conservation matters

Asian elephants face habitat loss and human–wildlife conflict. Protecting forest corridors and reducing conflict at the edges of protected areas are critical to their long-term survival.

Every ticket supports animal care, enrichment and conservation education at Gorakhpur Zoo.
