How it works

Three steps to the gate

1. Choose your date

Select a visiting date and how many adults, children or foreign visitors will join you.

2. Get instant pricing

The calculator applies entry rates and group discounts automatically. Confirm your details.

3. Show your QR ticket

Your booking ID and mock QR code appear instantly. Show it at the entrance gate.

Please note: This is a demonstration booking system. No real payment is taken and tickets are not issued by Gorakhpur Zoo. Bookings are stored only in your browser's localStorage. For actual group bookings, please contact the zoo office.
