The zoo is open from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Tuesday to Sunday. Last entry is at 4:30 PM and the ticket counter closes at 4:15 PM. The zoo is closed every Monday and on notified public holidays.

Indian visitors pay ₹50 for adults (over 12 years), ₹25 for children aged 6–12 and nothing for children below 6. Foreign visitors pay ₹400 (over 6 years) and children below 6 enter free. Add-ons such as the 7D theatre and sightseeing vehicle have separate rates.

Yes. Use our online booking page to choose a date and visitor counts, see live pricing and receive an instant booking ID and mock QR ticket. Please note this website is a demonstration project and no real payment is taken.

Yes. Groups of 20 or more receive 15% off entry tickets and groups of 50 or more receive 20% off. Discounts apply automatically in the booking calculator and cannot be combined with other offers.

Yes. Parking is available for cycles (₹5), two-wheelers (₹10), autos (₹30), cars (₹50), mini buses (₹150) and big buses (₹200) for up to four hours. Extra charges apply after four hours.

Outside food is allowed in designated picnic areas. Please do not take food into animal enclosures and never feed the animals. Sealed water bottles and light snacks are welcome.

The main visitor route is wheelchair-friendly and ramps are available at ticketing and the main attractions. Accessible washrooms are located near the entrance, and staff can assist at the gate.

No. For the safety of our animals and visitors, pets are not permitted inside the zoo grounds. Please do not leave pets in parked vehicles.

Personal photography requires a ₹100 daily ticket and videography is ₹500 daily. Commercial and movie shoots require prior written permission and additional fees. Flash photography is not allowed in the aquarium, and drones are prohibited.

The 7D theatre is a short immersive experience with motion seats, 3D visuals, wind and light effects. Tickets are ₹100 for children aged 6–12 and ₹150 for visitors over 12. It is not recommended for pregnant women or heart patients.
